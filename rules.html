Posting rules

Short list, applied to every post before it appears.

  1. No personal contact details. Phone numbers, emails and social handles are blocked automatically. Use the claim flow.
  2. Describe the item, not the person. Don't name or accuse anyone.
  3. Keep one secret detail back. Finders ask it, owners answer it. It's never public.
  4. Real items only. No selling, buying, ads or links.
  5. IDs and cards: hide the name and number. Say "ID card found" and let the owner describe it.
  6. Meet in public. Prefer the security desk or library entrance, in daylight hours.
  7. Posts expire after 60 days and move to the archive. Owners can mark an item returned sooner.
  8. Moderators can reject or remove posts that break these rules, with a reason shown on your manage page.

Safe hand-in points

Not comfortable meeting someone? Drop found items at one of these and say so in your post.

Security desk, Main Block

Open 8am to 8pm. Items are logged and held for 60 days.

Library help counter

Open 9am to 6pm. Best for books, cards and bags.
